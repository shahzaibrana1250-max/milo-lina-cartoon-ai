⬇ Download video

Recording captures animation + music + burned-in subtitles. Spoken voices play live during preview only — browsers cannot route device speech into a recording. This is an animatic (camera motion over still art), not AI-generated motion. For true AI motion, see the MiniMax H3 option below.

Character voices (free, device voices)

Available voices depend on your phone. Milo defaults to a higher pitch, Lina higher still, Bobo the highest and fastest. Change them here; they save automatically.

Real AI video (optional, paid)

MiniMax H3 is the best fit for these scenes: 5–15 seconds per clip, up to 9 reference images, native audio, about $0.08/second at 768p. No free tier — it needs your own key with balance. The adapter below is untested live (no key yet); if a browser call is blocked by CORS, use the exported prompt pack on the provider's website instead.
